## [2006] 能登半島為期四年地震序列壓力釋放後的群震終止與餘震驅動機制
**Swarm cessation and aftershock drivers following the pressure release of a four-year-long seismic sequence at the Noto Peninsula**

- 期刊：Earth and Planetary Science Letters (ScienceDirect)
- DOI：10.1016/j.epsl.2026.120113
- 原文連結：[連結](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0012821X26002967?dgcid=rss_sd_all)

**Summary (EN)**
The study examined seismic and aseismic processes during the 2024 Mw 7.5 Noto Peninsula sequence and, by comparing prior pore fluid pressure evolution with Coulomb stress changes from the 2023 Mw 6.3 sequence, concluded that elevated pore pressure likely played a larger role in triggering the 2024 mainshock than elastic triggering. Using a template-matching catalog and slip models, the authors found spatially varying drivers: static stress changes from the mainshock contributed to early seismicity (strongest in the southwest), the southwest and central zones show migrating afterslip, and northeastern seismicity is mainly driven by localized afterslip. Evidence of swarm cessation in the central zone indicates poroelastic rebound after pressure release, consistent with a cyclical fault-valving mechanism.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
本研究檢視了2024年Mw7.5能登半島序列中的地震與無震過程，並將先前族群活動的孔隙流體壓力演化與2023年Mw6.3序列的庫倫應力變化比較後，認為升高的孔隙壓力比彈性觸發在誘發2024年主震上可能扮演更重要的角色。使用模板比對目錄與滑動模型，結果顯示物理驅動具有空間差異：主震的靜態應力變化促成早期地震活動且在西南部影響最強；西南與中部區域呈現遷移性的餘滑，而東北部地震主要由局部餘滑驅動。中部區域觀測到族群停止的證據顯示在壓力釋放後出現孔彈性回彈，與循環性斷層閥機制相符。
