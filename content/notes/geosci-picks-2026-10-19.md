## [4334] 由分布式聲學感測（DAS）推導之地震動模型變異成分
**Components of Variability in Ground‐Motion Model Derived from Distributed Acoustic Sensing**

- 期刊：Geophysics (GeoScienceWorld) — Advance Access
- Published：Wed, 14 Oct 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0120260003/793479
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/bssa/article/doi/10.1785/0120260003/793479/Components-of-Variability-in-Ground-Motion-Model)

**Summary (EN)**
Distributed acoustic sensing (DAS) provides meter‑scale measurements along fiber cables that open new possibilities for ground‑motion models (GMMs) but also introduces DAS‑specific nonergodic effects (cable coupling, orientation) and channel correlation that complicate GMM development. This study presents the first regional, partially nonergodic DAS‑based GMM using strain‑rate data from a 400‑channel array in Hualien City, Taiwan (77 earthquakes, Mw 3.2–7.1), extending classical variability frameworks to include cable coupling, cable orientation, and Matérn‑kernel channel correlation. The resulting model reproduces magnitude–distance scaling comparable to classical GMMs, finds cable coupling as a broadband dominant source of within‑event variability while orientation contributes only a small amount, and shows that accounting for channel correlation substantially reduces channel‑related uncertainty, underscoring the importance of deployment metadata and correlation modeling for DAS‑based seismic hazard work.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
分布式聲學感測（DAS）能沿光纜提供公尺尺度的稠密量測，為地表動搖模型（GMM）帶來新機會，但也引入纜線耦合、方向性等特有非平穩成分及通道相關性，增加模型建置難度。本文以台灣花蓮市一組400通道陣列（77場地震，Mw 3.2–7.1）建立首個區域性、部分非平穩的DAS‑GMM，將變異性框架擴展為包含纜線耦合、纜線方向及以Matérn核描述的通道相關性。結果顯示該模型在震級–距離尺度上與傳統GMM相當，纜線耦合為頻帶寬廣且主導的事件內變異來源、方向性影響較小，且考慮通道相關性可顯著降低通道相關的不確定度，凸顯部署元資料與相關性建模的重要性。
