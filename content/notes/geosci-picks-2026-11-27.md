## [5011] 將關鍵供水管線轉化為地震斷層活動感測器
**Transforming Critical Water Pipelines Into Seismic Fault Movement Sensors**

- 期刊：JGR: Solid Earth (AGU/Wiley) — eTOC
- Published：Tue, 24 Nov 2026 05:28:43 -0800
- DOI：10.1029/2026GL123978
- 原文連結：[連結](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2026GL123978?af=R)

**Summary (EN)**
Researchers turned a fiber-optic-instrumented water pipeline crossing California’s Hayward Fault into a shape sensor. By combining distributed strain and temperature measurements with a rod-based reconstruction model, they measured pipeline deformation and captured aseismic fault slip of about 4 mm per year. This is the first reported field demonstration of long-term aseismic fault-slip monitoring using distributed fiber-optic shape sensing on a pipeline.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
研究人員將一條穿越加州海沃德斷層、配有光纖的水管改造成形狀感測器。透過結合分布式應變與溫度量測，以及基於 Cosserat 桿件的形狀重建模型，他們量測了管線變形，並捕捉到每年約 4 毫米的無震斷層滑移。這是首次有紀錄的現地示範，利用部署於穿越活動斷層管線上的分布式光纖形狀感測，長期監測無震斷層滑移。

## [5022] 地震學學生的需求：了解地震學學習生態系及其與地震學技能培養工作坊的關聯
**What Your Seismology Students Want: Understanding the Seismology Learning Ecosystem and Its Connection to the Seismology Skill Building Workshop**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Wed, 25 Nov 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220260204/800070
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220260204/800070/What-Your-Seismology-Students-Want-Understanding)

**Summary (EN)**
The Seismology Skill Building Workshop (SSBW) is a 12-week online program that teaches coding, software, and analysis skills, and has grown from 760 participants in its first offering to more than 2,300 in 2025. To assess how it fits into seismology education, this study analyzes seven introductory course syllabi and surveys instructors about course content, teaching methods, and scientific computing instruction. The authors use these findings to examine whether the SSBW addresses gaps in the learning ecosystem and to encourage reflection on seismology teaching.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
地震學技能培訓工作坊（SSBW）是一項為期 12 週的線上課程，教授程式設計、軟體與分析技能；參與人數從首屆的 760 人增至 2025 年的 2,300 多人。本研究分析七份地震學導論課程大綱，並調查授課教師，以了解課程內容、教學方法及科學計算的教學情形。作者藉此探討 SSBW 是否能補足地震學教育生態中的不足，並促進對地震學教學的反思。

## [5033] 由野外資料與無人航空器調查揭示的 2023 年 Mw 7.6 埃爾比斯坦地震（卡赫拉曼馬拉什，土耳其）地表破裂構造：EuQuaGe 任務小組調查成果
**Surface Rupture Architecture of the 2023 Mw 7.6 Elbistan Earthquake (Kahramanmaraş, Türkiye) Revealed by Field Data and UAS Surveys: Results From EuQuaGe Task‐Force Mission**

- 期刊：Tectonics (AGU/Wiley) — eTOC
- Published：Wed, 25 Nov 2026 21:38:06 -0800
- DOI：10.1029/2026TC009537
- 原文連結：[連結](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2026TC009537?af=R)

**Summary (EN)**
The Mw 7.6 Elbistan earthquake produced an approximately 150 km-long, left-lateral surface rupture with locally more than 9 m of displacement. Field observations combined with UAS and remote-sensing data reveal strong along-strike variation in rupture geometry, deformation width, and slip, associated with fault bends, stepovers, and interactions between fault strands. The field-based results complement satellite measurements, which may miss discrete offsets and distributed deformation, and provide information relevant to hazard assessment and paleoseismology.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
規模 Mw 7.6 的厄爾比斯坦地震形成約 150 公里長的左移走滑地表破裂，局部位移超過 9 公尺。結合野外觀測、無人航空系統（UAS）與遙測資料，研究發現破裂幾何、變形帶寬度及滑移量沿破裂帶變化顯著，且與斷層彎曲、階區及斷層分支交互作用有關。野外資料補足了衛星觀測可能未能辨識的離散位移與分散變形，並可為斷層位移危害評估及古地震研究提供參考。
