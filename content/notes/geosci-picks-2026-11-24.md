## [4646] 結合地震訊號尺度圖與相位檢測機率，提升深度學習模型分類局部爆炸與地震的泛化能力
**Combining Seismic Scalograms and Phase Detection Probabilities to Improve Generalization of Deep Learning Classification of Local Explosions and Earthquakes**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220260235/799355
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220260235/799355/Combining-Seismic-Scalograms-and-Phase-Detection)

**Summary (EN)**
This study compared convolutional neural networks that classify local earthquakes and shallow borehole explosions using seismic scalograms alone or scalograms combined with P- and S-wave detection probabilities from an existing model. The two-branch model achieved slightly higher balanced accuracy on data from 16 localities (95.7% versus 94.7%), while using about half as many parameters and converging faster. Its advantage was greater when tested on localities excluded from training: average accuracy rose from 84.4% to 87.6%, indicating that phase-detection probabilities can help classification models generalize to new regions.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
本研究比較兩種卷積神經網路，利用地震時頻圖，或結合既有模型產生的 P 波與 S 波到時偵測機率，來分類局部地震與淺層鑽孔爆炸。使用 16 個地區資料測試時，雙分支模型的平衡準確率略高於單分支模型（95.7% 對 94.7%），參數量約減半，且訓練收斂較快。測試未納入訓練的地區時，雙分支模型的平均準確率由 84.4% 提升至 87.6%，顯示相位偵測機率有助於模型應用至新地區時維持泛化能力。

## [4841] 地震源時間函數形狀的物理控制因素
**Physical Controls on the Shape of Earthquake Source Time Functions**

- 期刊：Earth and Space Science (AGU/Wiley) — eTOC
- Published：Sat, 14 Nov 2026 17:56:41 -0800
- DOI：10.1029/2026JB034700
- 原文連結：[連結](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2026JB034700?af=R)

**Summary (EN)**
Spectral boundary-integral simulations show that different physical mechanisms shape distinct features of earthquake source time functions: rupture initiation location controls growth curvature and symmetry, background stress gradients control how abruptly rupture ends, and overstress-zone geometry influences plateau development. These structural factors do not produce the pronounced early peak and long tail often observed in natural earthquakes; such tails emerge when the secondary frictional weakening distance is comparable to or greater than total fault slip. The mechanisms are largely separable, though not fully additive in peak timing, and their combinations may help explain observed STF diversity and inform seismic source inversion and near-fault ground-motion prediction.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
譜域邊界積分模擬顯示，不同物理機制會影響地震震源時間函數的不同特徵：破裂起始位置控制成長曲率與對稱性，背景應力梯度控制破裂停止的急緩，而超應力區的幾何形狀則影響平台期的形成。這些結構因素無法產生自然地震中常見的顯著早峰與長尾；當次級摩擦弱化距離接近或超過斷層總滑移量時，才會出現明顯長尾。各機制的影響大致可分，但對峰值時間並非完全可加；它們的組合或可解釋震源時間函數的多樣性，並有助於地震源反演及近斷層地動預測。

## [4858] 地震物理、地震預報及其他專題導言——1975 年 Mₛ 7.3 海城地震 50 年後
**Introduction to the Special Section on Earthquake Physics, Earthquake Forecasting, and Beyond—50 Yr After the 1975     M  s     7.3 Haicheng Earthquake**

- 期刊：Geophysics (GeoScienceWorld) — Advance Access
- Published：Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0120260281/799784
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/bssa/article/doi/10.1785/0120260281/799784/Introduction-to-the-Special-Section-on-Earthquake)

**Summary (EN)**
The 1975 magnitude 7.3 Haicheng earthquake is widely regarded as a successful major-earthquake prediction, with an evacuation believed to have saved hundreds of thousands of lives. Months of varied, geographically dispersed anomalies were observed, but short-term foreshocks—including a localized rise in seismic activity and a magnitude 5.1 event on the day—helped identify the likely location and approximate timing. The government evacuated about one million residents before the earthquake struck that evening.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
1975年海城規模7.3地震被視為成功預測大型地震的案例，當時的疏散行動被認為挽救了數十萬人的性命。主震前數月出現多種分布廣泛的異常現象；然而，短期前震，包括局部地震活動增加，以及當天發生的規模5.1地震，有助於判定可能的震央位置與大致發震時間。政府隨後疏散約100萬名居民，當晚地震發生。

## [4928] Quest碳捕集與封存計畫誘發的規模4地震
**The M4 Earthquakes Induced by the Quest Carbon Capture and Storage Project**

- 期刊：JGR: Solid Earth (AGU/Wiley) — eTOC
- Published：Wed, 18 Nov 2026 06:00:20 -0800
- DOI：10.1029/2026GL126186
- 原文連結：[連結](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2026GL126186?af=R)

**Summary (EN)**
The Quest carbon capture and storage project in Canada has injected about 1 million tonnes of CO₂ per year since 2015, while nearby basement faults have produced induced earthquakes up to magnitude 4. The earthquakes show a strong temporal relationship with injection, and the M4 events—the largest known to be induced by a CCS project—were widely felt in Edmonton. Modeling indicates small pressure increases at the distant earthquake area, but validation requires more data; the findings support careful monitoring and stronger risk assessment for CCS projects.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
加拿大 Quest 碳捕集與封存計畫自 2015 年起每年注入約 100 萬噸二氧化碳，同期附近基盤岩層斷層發生了最大規模達 4 級的誘發地震。地震與注入活動具有高度時間關聯性；這些 4 級地震是目前已知由碳封存計畫誘發的最大地震，並在埃德蒙頓廣泛有感。模型顯示，距離注入區約 35 公里的地震區壓力增幅不大，但仍需更多資料驗證；研究指出，碳封存計畫需要審慎監測並加強風險評估。

## [4930] 帕克菲爾德的中強地震：特徵雪花模型
**Moderate Earthquakes at Parkfield: A Characteristic Snowflake Model**

- 期刊：Geophysics (GeoScienceWorld) — Advance Access
- Published：Thu, 19 Nov 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0120260184/799873
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/bssa/article/doi/10.1785/0120260184/799873/Moderate-Earthquakes-at-Parkfield-A-Characteristic)

**Summary (EN)**
This review examines historical and recent earthquakes on the Parkfield segment of the San Andreas Fault, including ground-shaking evidence from the 1966, 2004, and earlier mainshocks. The shaking patterns indicate rupture directivity, including compelling evidence that the 1901 earthquake ruptured northwestward, so the 2004 event’s northwestward rupture was not unique. The findings support a characteristic-earthquake model in which events are broadly similar but vary in slip pattern, rupture direction, and possibly magnitude; ruptures tend to be unilateral and nucleate near either end of the segment. The authors also conclude that the 1860, 1877, and 1885 earthquakes did not occur on the fault’s creeping section.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
本文回顧聖安德烈亞斯斷層帕克菲爾德段的歷史與近期地震，並檢視包括 1966 年、2004 年及更早主震的地面震動資料。震動分布顯示破裂具有方向性，其中有力證據指出 1901 年地震向西北破裂，因此 2004 年地震向西北破裂並非特例。研究結果支持特徵地震模型：帕克菲爾德主震大致相似，但滑移型態、破裂方向及可能的規模各有不同；破裂多半為單側，且常從該段兩端附近起始。作者也認為，1860、1877 與 1885 年的地震並非發生於斷層的蠕滑段。
