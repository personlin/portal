## [2450] 利用密集陣列資料之環境噪聲層析成像揭示東台灣地下剪切波速度沿走向之構造驅動變化
**Tectonically Driven Along‐Strike Variations in Subsurface Shear‐Wave Velocity in Eastern Taiwan Revealed by Ambient Noise Tomography Using Dense Arrays Data**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Tue, 23 Jun 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220250418/731957
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220250418/731957/Tectonically-Driven-Along-Strike-Variations-in)

**Summary (EN)**
The authors deployed five temporary arrays (411 stations) across eastern Taiwan and used ambient noise tomography to image 3D shear‑wave velocity to 6 km depth. They find pronounced north–south differences: southern low‑velocity zones are about twice as thick as northern ones and have near‑surface velocities below 1.0 km/s, which they attribute to footwall subsidence and deeper fluvial sediments along the Longitudinal Valley fault, abundant fluids/gases, and the sedimentary Lichi Formation (which may extend northward to ~23.5°N). Fault‑zone velocity patterns are relatively uniform in the north but in the south can either align with or cut across velocity contours. These variations are consistent with other geophysical, geological, and geodetic observations, linking surface geology with deeper tectonic structure.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
研究團隊於2016–2019年間佈設五個臨時陣列（共411個站），利用環境雜訊層析成像出上方6公里的三維剪切波速度構造。結果顯示明顯的南北差異：南部低速帶厚度約為北部的兩倍，近地表速度低於1.0 km/s，作者推測成因包括縱谷斷層下盤的持續下陷與深部河流堆積物、下盤含豐富流體與氣體，以及利吉層沉積物（利吉層可能向北延伸至約23.5°N）。斷層帶速度在北部對比不明顯，而南部則有時與速度等值線一致或垂直切穿，這些南北差異與其他地球物理、地質與測地資料相互呼應，將地表過程與深部構造連結起來。

## [2452] 以廣義平均破裂距離推導之大型俯衝板界面地震地震動模型
**A Ground‐Motion Model Derived Using a Generalized Mean Rupture Distance for Large Slab Interface Earthquakes**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Tue, 23 Jun 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220250155/731955
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220250155/731955/A-Ground-Motion-Model-Derived-Using-a-Generalized)

**Summary (EN)**
The authors evaluate a generalized mean rupture distance Rp, derived from distributed slip models (DSMs), for improving ground‐motion models (GMMs) of large slab interface earthquakes by recalibrating the path term of an existing GMM. The Rp‑based recalibration fits the data at least as well as using the traditional Rrup, and using full slip information or trimmed DSM geometry is preferable to assuming uniform slip on an extended fault. They find Rp tuned to reduce model uncertainty is nearer the maximum rupture distance for peak ground velocity (PGV) than for acceleration, and conclude that incorporating slip‑derived moment release via Rp could improve slab‑interface GMMs, especially with a larger, more geographically diverse dataset.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
作者評估由分佈滑移模型（DSM）導出的廣義平均破裂距離 Rp，用以改進大型板塊界面地震的地面運動模型，透過重新校準既有模型的路徑項來進行測試。以 Rp 重新校準後的模型至少與使用傳統 Rrup 的改校模型同樣符合資料，且完整使用滑移資訊或裁剪 DSM 幾何比假設均勻滑移更理想。研究發現為減少模型不確定性而調整的 Rp 在峰值地面速度（PGV）上更接近最大破裂距離，並指出透過 Rp 納入滑移衍生的矩釋放，配合更多地理分布更廣的地震資料，可望改進板塊界面事件的 GMM。

## [2463] 一種基於DTS估算永久安裝光纖至套管中心線徑向距離的方法
**A DTS-based method for estimating the radial distance of permanently installed optical fiber from the casing centerline**

- 期刊：GSA Bulletin — Advance Access
- Published：Wed, 24 Jun 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1190/GEO-2025-0408/732111
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/seg/geophysics/article/doi/10.1190/GEO-2025-0408/732111/A-DTS-based-method-for-estimating-the-radial)

**Summary (EN)**
The paper presents a method to estimate the radial distance of permanently installed optical fiber from the casing centerline using Distributed Temperature Sensing (DTS) data. It inverts depth-specific temperature responses during heating events to derive the fiber’s distance and validates these inversion results with Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) data recorded during hydraulic fracturing. The estimated radial distances are used to assess fiber coupling along the well and to correct biased production DTS measurements caused by variable fiber positioning.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
本研究提出一種利用分布式溫度感測（DTS）資料反推永久安裝光纖與套管中心線之徑向距離的方法。方法透過分析加熱事件中不同深度的溫度響應進行反演，並以同一光纖於水力壓裂期間記錄的分布式聲學感測（DAS）資料驗證反演結果。推得的徑向距離可用於評估井段間光纖耦合情況，並修正因光纖位置變異導致的生產DTS量測偏差。
