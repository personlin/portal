## [2117] 藉由測地觀測揭示的 2025 年緬甸曼德勒 Mw 7.7 地震之同震滑移模型與震間應變累積
**Coseismic Slip Model and Interseismic Strain Accumulation of the 2025     M  w     7.7 Mandalay, Myanmar, Earthquake Revealed by Geodetic Observations**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Wed, 03 Jun 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220250370/731453
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220250370/731453/Coseismic-Slip-Model-and-Interseismic-Strain)

**Summary (EN)**
On 28 March 2025 a Mw 7.7 earthquake ruptured the central and southern Sagaing fault, producing a surface rupture over 500 km, the longest documented for a strike‑slip event. Sentinel‑1A/B SAR images were processed with pixel offset tracking and combined with differential interferometry and aperture interferometry to retrieve high‑resolution coseismic displacements and quantify on‑ and off‑fault deformation. The rupture was dominated by right‑lateral strike‑slip with a maximum slip of ~6 m in the shallow portion, and the shallow slip deficit is reported to be small.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
2025年3月28日，一場Mw7.7地震橫跨薩加因斷層中南段，產生超過500公里的地表破裂，為走滑型事件中記錄到最長的破裂。研究以Sentinel‑1A/B合成孔徑雷達影像與像素偏移追蹤(POT)，並整合差分干涉及孔徑干涉，回推高解析度同震位移並量化斷層近場與離斷層變形。結果顯示破裂以右旋走滑為主，淺部最大滑動約6公尺，且淺層滑脫缺小。

## [2120] 在地震波傳播的運算子學習中強制執行互惠原理
**Enforcing Reciprocity in Operator Learning for Seismic Wave Propagation**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Wed, 03 Jun 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220260082/731450
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220260082/731450/Enforcing-Reciprocity-in-Operator-Learning-for)

**Summary (EN)**
The authors present a transformer-based neural operator that hard-codes the physical reciprocity principle to model seismic wave propagation. By using cross-attention and commutative operations the model is invariant to swapping source and receiver positions, supports simultaneous multiple-source realizations, and offers an order-of-magnitude inference speedup at similar memory cost compared to a conventional neural operator in realistic multisource setups. They demonstrate the approach on particle velocity fields under single forces and note it can be applied to pressure fields from dilatational sources and to travel-time fields governed by the eikonal equation.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
作者提出一種基於 Transformer 的神經算子，將波動傳播中的互易性原理以硬編碼方式納入模型中。透過交互注意力與交換性運算，模型在交換震源與接收器位置時保持不變，並支援多震源同時推理，在真實的多震源情境下，相較於傳統神經算子在類似記憶體開銷下達到約一個量級的推理加速。作者以單力作用下的粒子速度場示範該方法，並指出此架構亦可應用於膨脹源的壓力場及由 eikonal 方程支配的行進時間場。

## [2121] 利用全球滑坡誘發之地震事件目錄的地震波形特徵估算滑坡體積
**Landslide Volume Estimation from Seismic Waveform Features Using a Global Catalog of Seismogenic Events**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Wed, 03 Jun 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220260018/731449
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220260018/731449/Landslide-Volume-Estimation-from-Seismic-Waveform)

**Summary (EN)**
The authors develop a globally applicable method to estimate landslide volume from seismic waveform features, aiming to enable rapid characterization of remote events using existing seismic networks. They train multivariable linear regression models on five seismic features derived from 129 landslides with independently estimated volumes and present two preferred models, including one for smaller landslides lacking long‐period signals. The best model, for landslides >100,000 m3 with observable long‐period energy, uses only LP (20–100 s) absolute maximum amplitude and HF (1–5 Hz) rise time, achieves an R2 of 0.79, and predicted volumes within one order of magnitude for 55 of 58 events, mitigating limitations of amplitude‐only approaches.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
作者提出一種可在全球範圍應用的地震波特徵估算崩塌體積方法，目的在於利用既有地震監測網路快速表徵偏遠事件。研究以129個具有獨立體積估計的崩塌事件為訓練資料，使用五個地震特徵進行多變量線性迴歸，並提出兩個首選模型（含一個適用於未觀測到長週期訊號的小型崩塌）。表現最佳的模型適用於大於100,000 m3且含有長週期能量的事件，只需LP（20–100 s）絕對最大振幅與HF（1–5 Hz）上升時間兩個特徵，R2為0.79，對55/58個事件的體積估計達到一個量級內，能減少僅以振幅為基礎方法受質量與加速度變化影響的限制。

## [2144] 沿離岸 CASTOR 天然氣儲存場光纖電纜的地震環境噪聲成像
**Seismic ambient noise imaging along the fibre-optic cable of the offshore CASTOR gas storage field**

- 期刊：GJI (OUP) — RSS
- Published：Thu, 28 May 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1093/gji/ggag197
- 原文連結：[連結](https://academic.oup.com/gji/article/doi/10.1093/gji/ggag197/8699870?rss=1)

**Summary (EN)**
The authors used continuous distributed acoustic sensing (DAS) recordings on a 30-km dark fibre linking the offshore CASTOR gas storage field to land to extract empirical Green’s functions from ambient seismic noise. Using wavelet phase cross-correlation and phase-weighted stacking, they recovered Scholte waves on marine channels and Rayleigh waves on land channels, and observed weak reflections from the basin edge. A 2-D shear-velocity model derived from phase-velocity measurements and MCMC depth inversions images the sedimentary basin to depths exceeding 1 km, identifies the Amposta Central Fault and basement at ~1 km depth, and refines the offshore velocity model for improved seismic monitoring and hazard assessment in an area with induced seismicity.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
作者在連接近海 CASTOR 天然氣存儲場與陸地的 30 公里暗光纖上使用分佈式聲學感測 (DAS) 的連續記錄，從環境地震雜訊中擷取經驗格林函數。透過小波相位互相關與時頻相位加權疊加，於海域通道主要回收到 Scholte 基模與第一高次模，在陸域通道則出現 Rayleigh 基模，並觀察到來自盆地邊緣的弱反射波場。以 Scholte 波的相速度觀測配合 MCMC 深度反演得到的 2D 剪切速度模型，可解析超淺水飽和沉積物至超過 1 公里深度，識別出 Amposta 中央斷層及其以西約 1 公里深的基底，並在有誘發地震活動的區域改善離岸速度模型以利監測與震害評估。

## [2145] 用一步預測隨機森林進行誘發地震危害預測：以中國四川盆地南部瀘縣地區為例
**One-step prediction random forest for induced seismic hazard forecasting: application to the Luxian area, Southern Sichuan Basin, China**

- 期刊：GJI (OUP) — RSS
- Published：Fri, 22 May 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1093/gji/ggag188
- 原文連結：[連結](https://academic.oup.com/gji/article/doi/10.1093/gji/ggag188/8691065?rss=1)

**Summary (EN)**
The paper develops a One-Step Prediction random forest framework to forecast induced seismic hazard by modeling spatiotemporal links between hydraulic fracturing activities, geology, past seismicity, and future seismic events. It uses a seismic-energy labeling scheme to estimate potential energy release and employs feature attribution (SHAP) to identify dominant geological and operational drivers. The authors conceptually extend the conventional traffic-light risk system into a traffic-light hazard management scheme informed by risk theory and evaluate predictive performance across hazard levels using confusion matrices.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
本研究提出一種 One-Step Prediction 隨機森林框架，用以建模水力壓裂井部署、地質特徵、歷史地震與未來地震發生的時空關係，以進行誘發地震危害預測。研究採用基於一步預測的地震能量標籤法估計潛在能量釋放，並透過 SHAP 特徵歸因識別主要的地質與操作驅動因子。作者將傳統交通信號燈風險系統概念性延伸為結合風險理論的交通信號燈危害管理方案，並以混淆矩陣評估不同危害情境下的預測表現。

## [2183] 透過基於 ETAS 的合成目錄將地震序列納入國家地震危險度模型：義大利案例
**Including Earthquake Sequences on National Seismic Hazard Models Through ETAS‐Based Synthetic Catalogs: The Case of Italy**

- 期刊：Geophysics (GeoScienceWorld) — Advance Access
- Published：Wed, 03 Jun 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0120260024/731448
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/bssa/article/doi/10.1785/0120260024/731448/Including-Earthquake-Sequences-on-National-Seismic)

**Summary (EN)**
Probabilistic seismic hazard analysis typically assumes a time‑invariant Poisson process and removes aftershocks by declustering, which is subjective and may underestimate hazard because aftershocks can contribute substantially to ground shaking. This study uses the SimplETAS algorithm to generate many 50‑year ETAS‐based synthetic catalogs, assigns seismogenic structures to events, and computes hazard curves for four Italian cities without assuming Poissonian mainshocks, comparing results to the national MPS19 model and its sequence‑corrected version (MPS19_cluster). Results show that explicitly modeling earthquake sequences raises estimated hazard at all sites, that SimplETAS and MPS19_cluster generally agree (even at short return periods) except in low‑hazard Milano where they diverge due to different spatial magnitude distributions, and that the ETAS approach allows flexible forecast windows and naturally captures spatial correlation of risk.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
傳統的機率地震危害分析多假定主震為時間不變的 Poisson 過程並以去耦程序移除餘震，這一做法主觀且可能低估因餘震造成的震動危害。本文使用 SimplETAS 產生大量 50 年的 ETAS 合成目錄，為每一震源分配合理的成震結構，並在不假定 Poisson 的情況下為義大利四座城市計算危害曲線，與國家模型 MPS19 及其序列修正版 MPS19_cluster 比較。結果顯示明確建模地震序列會提高所有地點的危害估計，SimplETAS 與 MPS19_cluster 大致一致（包含短重現期），但在地震危害最低的米蘭出現分歧，反映兩模型在震級空間變異上的差異；此外 ETAS 方法可靈活選擇預測時窗並自然捕捉風險的空間相關性。

## [2223] 統一 Gutenberg–Richter 定律與機率性目錄完整度
**Unifying the Gutenberg–Richter Law with Probabilistic Catalog Completeness**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Mon, 08 Jun 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220250289/731658
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220250289/731658/Unifying-the-Gutenberg-Richter-Law-with)

**Summary (EN)**
The authors replace the abrupt completeness threshold traditionally used in seismic catalogs with a smooth probabilistic transition, introducing four augmented Gutenberg–Richter (GR) models that embed incompleteness into the complementary cumulative frequency–magnitude distribution (CCFMD) via two parameters: mc (transition magnitude) and σc (transition range). They validate the models using synthetic and real catalogs within a framework that tests correct specification and misspecification, and find the GRAE model—which allows asymmetric convergence around mc—consistently outperforms the others, yielding more robust parameter estimates such as the b value. The approach unifies incompleteness modeling in the CCFMD, gives parameters clear physical/statistical meaning, captures nonlinear and asymmetric detection behaviors, and enables probabilistic inclusion of small events to improve seismic hazard assessments.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
作者以平滑的機率過渡取代傳統的尖銳完備度閾值，提出四種擴展的 Gutenberg–Richter（GR）模型，將不完備性透過兩個參數 mc（過渡震級）與 σc（從不完備到完備的過渡範圍）嵌入互補累積頻度–震級分布（CCFMD）。在包含正確指定與錯誤指定測試的驗證框架下，利用合成與實際目錄進行評估，發現允許在 mc 兩側不對稱收斂的 GRAE 模型表現最佳，能比既有方法更穩健地估計如 b 值等參數。此方法統一了 CCFMD 中的不完備性建模，使參數具物理和統計意義、能捕捉非線性及不對稱的偵測行為，並可用機率性加權納入過去被捨去的小震以改進地震危險性評估。
