## [2024] 邁向具有三維低頻合成波的 SCEC 寬頻平台 (BBP 3D)
**Toward an SCEC Broadband Platform with 3D Low‐Frequency Synthetics (BBP 3D)**

- 期刊：Geophysics (GeoScienceWorld) — Advance Access
- Published：Wed, 27 May 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0120250270/731186
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/bssa/article/doi/10.1785/0120250270/731186/Toward-an-SCEC-Broadband-Platform-with-3D-Low)

**Summary (EN)**
The Broadband Platform (BBP) currently uses 1D, region-specific velocity models for low-frequency ground-motion simulations, which can miss effects from 3D sedimentary basins and complex topography and thus introduce inaccuracies. The authors implemented a BBP 3D that replaces the 1D low-frequency simulations with 3D physics-based simulations including topography and tested it on the 1989 Loma Prieta, 1996 Northridge, and 2019 Ridgecrest events using source ensembles. BBP 3D reduced the 0.2–1 Hz mean Fourier amplitude spectral (FAS) bias by as much as 89% (largest on the vertical component), produced the biggest improvements in populated basin areas (with topography explaining up to 50% of the gain), and increased goodness-of-fit for median 0.2–1 Hz duration and cumulative absolute velocity by as much as 79% and 70%, respectively, with smaller improvements between 1 and 2 Hz.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
現有的 Broadband Platform (BBP) 以一維、區域最佳化的速度模型計算低頻地動，無法完整呈現三維沉積盆地與複雜地形的影響，可能導致結果不準確。作者開發並測試了 BBP 3D，將一維低頻模擬替換為包含地形的三維物理模擬，對 1989 年 Loma Prieta、1996 年 Northridge 與 2019 年 Ridgecrest 事件進行源集成測試。BBP 3D 在 0.2–1 Hz 頻段將平均傅立葉振幅譜（FAS）偏差最多降低至 89%，垂直分量改善最大；在人口稠密的盆地區域改善最顯著（地形貢獻最多約 50%），且中位數 0.2–1 Hz 持續時間與累積絕對速度的擬合度分別提高最多約 79% 與 70%，在 1–2 Hz 頻段的改善則較小。
