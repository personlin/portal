## [551] 月球採礦試驗場址：de Gerlache、Shackleton 與 Sverdrup–Henson 區域資源開採機會與挑戰之綜述
**Test mining sites at the moon: A review of resource extraction opportunities and challenges at de Gerlache, Shackleton, and Sverdrup-Henson Regions**

- 期刊：Earth-Science Reviews (ScienceDirect)
- DOI：10.1016/j.enggeo.2026.108628
- 原文連結：[連結](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0013795226000876?dgcid=rss_sd_all)

**Summary (EN)**
This paper evaluates the feasibility of nine candidate sites in the de Gerlache–Shackleton region of the lunar south pole for future mining and extraction activities by comparing geological characteristics, surface slopes, illumination, permanently shadowed regions, thermal environments, and communication constraints. The authors introduce a preliminary optimization-based scoring framework to rank sites by resource potential, engineering constraints, and geologic hazards. Results highlight trade-offs between resource accessibility and operational risk, identify locations that could serve as early testbeds for lunar mining demonstrations, and promote an engineering-geology approach to environmentally informed, transferable site-assessment planning.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
本文對月球南極de Gerlache–Shackleton區域的九個候選地點進行可行性評估，比較地質特性、地表坡度、照明情況、永久陰影區、熱環境與通訊限制，以判斷其支援未來採礦與資源取出的潛力。作者提出一個初步的優化評分架構，用以根據資源潛力、工程限制與地質危害對場址進行排序。研究指出資源可及性與營運風險間的關鍵權衡，標出可作為早期採礦示範測試場的地點，並主張以工程地質視角推進兼顧環境的可轉移場址評估方法。
