## [2355] Kappa 是什麼？
**What Is Kappa?**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Tue, 16 Jun 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220260050/731805
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220260050/731805/What-Is-Kappa)

**Summary (EN)**
The paper describes the constant-stress-drop omega-square earthquake source model, originally proposed by Aki and combined with Brune’s size-dependent corner frequency. In this single corner-frequency point-source model the far-field Fourier acceleration spectrum rises with the square of frequency up to the corner frequency and is flat above it. This Aki–Brune formulation is commonly referred to in engineering as the seismological model.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
本文說明了常應力降的ω^2震源模型，該模型由Aki提出並與Brune的依地震規模變化之轉折頻率相結合。此單一轉折頻率的點源模型中，遠場傅立葉加速度譜在轉折頻率之前隨頻率的平方增加，而在轉折頻率之上則維持恆定。該Aki–Brune表述在工程上常被稱為地震學模型。

## [2356] AutoBETA：全自動地震目錄補完工具
**AutoBETA: Fully Automated Earthquake Catalog Replenishment Tool**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Tue, 16 Jun 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220250253/731804
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220250253/731804/AutoBETA-Fully-Automated-Earthquake-Catalog)

**Summary (EN)**
The paper presents AutoBETA, a fully automated tool for replenishing incomplete earthquake catalogs that combines grid-based density analysis with flexible missing-region identification. It accepts user-defined polygons and hybrid union/intersection strategies to incorporate expert knowledge. AutoBETA was tested on one synthetic case and four real sequences (2008 Mw7.9 Wenchuan, 2024 Mw7.5 Noto, 2010 M5.26 Baja California, and 2016 M6.06 Norcia) at magnitude thresholds M≥3.0, M≥2.0, M≥1.0, and M≥2.0; after 100 automated iterations it replenished 43.9%, 40.0%, 40.5%, and 44.8% of the final catalogs, substantially improving completeness for aftershock analysis.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
本文提出 AutoBETA，一套完全自動化的地震目錄補全工具，結合格網密度分析與彈性的缺失區域識別方法。此工具支援使用者定義多邊形及聯集/交集等混合策略以納入專家知識。作者在一個合成資料與四個實際序列（2008年汶川 Mw7.9、2024年能登 Mw7.5、2010年巴哈加利福尼亞 M5.26、2016年諾爾恰 M6.06）上以不同震級閾值（M≥3.0、M≥2.0、M≥1.0、M≥2.0）測試，100次自動疊代後分別補全了43.9%、40.0%、40.5%與44.8%的最終目錄，大幅提升目錄完整性以利餘震分析。

## [2361] 地震波速變化揭示山區流域的水文與生態水文過程
**Seismic Velocity Variations Illustrate Hydrological and Ecohydrological Processes in a Mountain Watershed**

- 期刊：JGR: Solid Earth (AGU/Wiley) — eTOC
- Published：Tue, 16 Jun 2026 02:52:04 -0700
- DOI：10.1029/2025GL121378
- 原文連結：[連結](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2025GL121378?af=R)

**Summary (EN)**
The study used passive seismic interferometry to measure seismic velocity changes (dv/v) at 2–4 Hz and 4–8 Hz from six stations along a hillslope in a snowmelt-driven semi-arid catchment between May and November 2023, comparing the results to precipitation, soil moisture, and groundwater time series. They found dv/v variations correlated with groundwater level and tracked seasonal catchment wetting and evapotranspiration, with stronger correlations on the cooler, wetter north-facing slope than on the warmer, drier south-facing slope. Differences between the two frequency bands point to depth-dependent pore-pressure changes and distinct hydrologic zones, showing dv/v can detect lateral water inputs, vertical water losses, and groundwater–vegetation interactions relevant for ecohydrological research and water management.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
本研究以被動地震干涉法在一個以融雪為主的半乾旱集水區，於2023年5月至11月沿山坡六個站點量測2–4 Hz與4–8 Hz的地震速度變化（dv/v），並與降雨、土壤含水量及地下水時間序列比較。研究發現dv/v變化與地下水位相關，並反映集水區的季節性增濕與蒸散作用；在較冷且較濕的北向山坡，dv/v與地下水的相關性強於較暖且較乾的南向山坡。兩個頻段的不同行為暗示孔隙壓力隨深度變化與不同的水文分層，顯示dv/v可用於偵測側向供水、垂直耗水及地下水─植被互動，對生態水文研究與水資源管理具參考價值。

## [2388] 艾特納火山自動偵測地震訊號之深度學習工作流程解析
**Insights into a Deep Learning Workflow for Automatic Detection of Seismic Signals at Mount Etna Volcano**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Thu, 18 Jun 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220250431/731845
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220250431/731845/Insights-into-a-Deep-Learning-Workflow-for)

**Summary (EN)**
The paper presents a workflow for automatic detection of volcanic seismic events using deep learning tools (PhaseNet and EQTransformer) via the SeisBench platform to pick P- and S-wave arrivals from continuous waveforms. Several pretrained models were systematically tested on Mount Etna data (Jan 2019–Jun 2020), showing that models trained on volcano-specific datasets outperform tectonic-only models; a modified frequency index was used to classify VT, LP, low-spectrum, mixed, and unclassified events with good agreement to reviewed catalogs. The results demonstrate strong potential for semi-automated volcanic monitoring but emphasize the need for site-specific model validation and expert oversight for reliable outcomes.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
本文提出一個利用深度學習工具（PhaseNet 和 EQTransformer）透過 SeisBench 平台，自動檢測火山地震並擷取 P、S 波到時的工作流程。研究系統性地測試多個預訓練模型於 2019 年 1 月到 2020 年 6 月的埃特納火山資料，發現火山專用訓練模型顯著優於僅以構造地震為訓練的模型；並以修改過的頻率指標將 VT、LP、低頻譜、混合與未分類事件分類，與已審核目錄高度一致。整體而言，此工作流程在埃特納的複雜環境下表現良好，但仍需地點特定的模型驗證與專家監督以確保結果可靠。
