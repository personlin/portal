## [2521] 含溫度修正的寬頻地震儀現場比較校準
**On‐Site Comparison Calibration of Broadband Seismometers with Temperature Correction**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Mon, 29 Jun 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220260117/732150
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220260117/732150/On-Site-Comparison-Calibration-of-Broadband)

**Summary (EN)**
The paper addresses how temperature-dependent sensitivity changes in reference seismometers degrade on-site comparison calibration accuracy and proposes a comparison calibration system with temperature correction. Applying temperature-based corrections improved reference sensitivity and allowed calibration of operational STS-1 seismometers, determining midband sensitivity around 0.5 Hz with uncertainties of 0.3%–0.5%, meeting observational network requirements. The study also discovered an unidentified 28 ms delay and characterized the relative frequency response down to 0.02 Hz, including a shift in cutoff frequency, contributing to the establishment of an on-site comparison calibration process.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
本文指出溫度依賴性會降低現場比較校正的準確性，並提出一種加入溫度校正的比較校正系統。經由溫度校正後提升了參考儀器靈敏度，並對運行中的STS-1地震儀進行校正，於約0.5 Hz處量得中頻靈敏度不確定度為0.3%–0.5%，符合觀測網的要求。研究同時發現一個未識別的28 ms延遲，並量化了至0.02 Hz的相對頻率響應及截止頻率的偏移，有助於建立現場比較校正流程。

## [2540] 透過統一機器學習地震目錄揭示伊斯帕尼奧拉島活躍的地殼與板片地震活動
**Active Crustal and Slab Seismicity in Hispaniola Revealed Through a Unified Machine‐Learning‐Derived Earthquake Catalog**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Wed, 01 Jul 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220260024/732203
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220260024/732203/Active-Crustal-and-Slab-Seismicity-in-Hispaniola)

**Summary (EN)**
The authors applied three deep‑learning phase pickers to continuous seismic data from 2014–2023 and used automated association and location tools to produce an aggregated machine‑learning earthquake catalog of over 47,000 events—roughly three times more than local network reports. Improved depth resolution and hypocenter relocations reveal a southeastward‑deepening seismicity trend consistent with subduction, concentrated low‑magnitude activity along major faults (Enriquillo–Plantain Garden and Septentrional–Oriente), and undercharacterized clusters near San José de Ocoa and Enriquillo Lake margins. Relocation with hypoDD images the subducting North American slab (potentially extending west to the Haiti–Dominican Republic border), shows diffuse seismicity near ~70°W, and eastward delineates a collision zone where a north‑dipping Caribbean slab meets a south‑dipping North American slab. The study demonstrates that ML‑based workflows can generate high‑quality seismic catalogs that better resolve slab geometries and tectonic processes, aiding seismic hazard assessment and real‑time monitoring in complex plate boundary regions.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
作者對2014–2023年的連續地震資料，使用三種深度學習相位挑選器並以自動化關聯與定位工具，合併出一個超過47,000個地震的機器學習目錄，約為地方網路報告量的三倍。改進的深度解析與重定位顯示與隱沒作用一致的向東南加深地震性趨勢，並捕捉到集中於主要斷層（Enriquillo–Plantain Garden與Septentrional–Oriente）的低震級活動，以及位於San José de Ocoa和Enriquillo湖邊界的先前未充分描述群集。使用hypoDD重定位後，影像化出可能向西延伸至海地—多明尼加邊界的北美板塊隱沒體，並在約70°W處顯示散漫性地震性，東側則界定出北傾的加勒比板塊與南傾的北美板塊相遇的碰撞帶。研究顯示機器學習流程能產生高品質地震目錄，協助解析板片構造並改善震害評估與即時監測。

## [2542] 數位典藏員：使用大型語言模型自動化處理舊有宏觀地震資料
**The Digital Archivist: Automating Legacy Macroseismic Data Processing Using Large Language Models**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Wed, 01 Jul 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220250362/732201
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220250362/732201/The-Digital-Archivist-Automating-Legacy)

**Summary (EN)**
The paper presents a workflow that uses Google’s Gemini 2.5 Pro large language model to automatically extract and structure macroseismic observations from inconsistent legacy reports. Applied to the 22 March 1957 M5.3 Daly City earthquake (and additionally to the 1971 ML6.7 Sylmar event), the method extracted addresses, assigned modified Mercalli intensities and descriptions, geocoded entries via Google’s API, and produced over 2,300 geocoded intensity reports and a shaking intensity map comparable in some ways to modern crowd‑sourced maps. The authors conclude that LLMs provide a scalable way to digitize large, unstructured macroseismic archives to support ground‑motion modeling and studies of site effects in urban areas.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
本文提出一種以 Google 的 Gemini 2.5 Pro 大型語言模型自動從不一致的歷史報告中擷取並結構化宏觀震感資料的工作流程。此方法應用於1957年3月22日的戴利城（M5.3）地震（並示範性地處理1971年Sylmar地震），擷取地址、原先指派的修正墨卡利烈度及描述，並透過 Google 地理編碼 API 進行定位，產生超過2300筆已地理編碼的烈度報告與可與現代群眾回報地圖相媲美的震感分布圖。作者認為 LLM 可作為可擴展的工具快速數位化大量非結構化的宏觀震感檔案，進而支援地面運動模型與城市場址效應研究。
