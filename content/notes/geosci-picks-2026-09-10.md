## [3693] 比較反演模型與速率—狀態地震模擬器（RSQSim）所產生之斷層內地震活動與機率地震危害：來自 Aotearoa 紐西蘭的見解
**Comparing On‐Fault Seismicity and Probabilistic Seismic Hazard from Inversion Models and the Rate and State Earthquake Simulator (RSQSim); Insights from Aotearoa New Zealand**

- 期刊：Geophysics (GeoScienceWorld) — Advance Access
- Published：Tue, 08 Sep 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0120260140/767612
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/bssa/article/doi/10.1785/0120260140/767612/Comparing-On-Fault-Seismicity-and-Probabilistic)

**Summary (EN)**
The paper compares on‑fault seismicity and probabilistic seismic hazard in Aotearoa New Zealand from a physics‑based RSQSim synthetic catalog and the inversion fault model (IFM) used in NZ NSHM 2022, both built on the New Zealand Community Fault Model. The IFM implies faults participate in larger magnitude ruptures than RSQSim, though RSQSim’s magnitude–frequency distribution lies within the IFM’s 5th–95th percentile uncertainty; peak ground acceleration differences for a 10% in 50 year level can be as large as about 250%, especially near low‑slip‑rate faults and above the Hikurangi–Kermadec subduction interface. Adding the NZ NSHM 2022 distributed seismicity model reduces discrepancies in low‑seismicity areas but not over the Hikurangi subduction zone; the authors note that incorporating physics into rupture forecasts is promising but raises challenges in handling uncertainty and testing simulators in regions with short historical records.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
本研究比較以物理為基礎的 RSQSim 合成地震目錄與 NZ NSHM 2022 所用的反演斷層模型（IFM）在紐西蘭在斷層上的地震性與機率性地震危害估計。結果顯示 IFM 表示斷層會參與較大震級的破裂，而 RSQSim 的震級–頻率分布仍落在 IFM 的 5%–95% 不確定度範圍內；在 50 年內 10% 超越機率水準下，二者在峰值地面加速度上的差異可達約 250%，尤其發生在低滑移速率斷層或位於希庫蘭吉—凱爾馬克德隱沒帶上方的東北島地區。將 NZ NSHM 2022 的分佈性地震模型納入比較後，低地震活動地區的差異有所減少，但在希庫蘭吉隱沒帶上方仍存在；作者指出將「物理」納入破裂預報具吸引力，但在處理不確定性與在歷史紀錄短的地區驗證模擬器方面仍有挑戰。
