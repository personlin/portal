## [2243] 藉由分布式聲學感測揭示墨西哥城垂直斷層的水震相互作用
**Hydroseismic interplays on perpendicular faults in Mexico City revealed by distributed acoustic sensing**

- 期刊：GJI (OUP) — RSS
- Published：Mon, 01 Jun 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1093/gji/ggag199
- 原文連結：[連結](https://academic.oup.com/gji/article/doi/10.1093/gji/ggag199/8699184?rss=1)

**Summary (EN)**
Using 15 months of continuous distributed acoustic sensing (DAS) on metropolitan telecom fiber in Mexico City, the study maps detailed wave propagation and precisely localizes local earthquakes. By employing realistic seismic velocity models the authors show limitations of constant Vp/Vs assumptions and Wadati diagrams in sedimentary basins. They document hydroseismic coupling where intense early-season rainfall coinciding with low aquifer levels can trigger moderate (Mw ≈ 3.5) earthquakes that induce slow slip on local faults and perpendicular secondary seismicity driven mainly by stress imbalance, and identify basin-converted and conical phases as major contributors to ground shaking that should be integrated into urban hazard assessments and building codes.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
本研究利用墨西哥城城市電信光纜上15個月的連續分佈式聲學感測（DAS）資料，揭示波動傳播的細節並精準定位局部地震。作者採用實際地震速率模型，指出在沉積盆地中以恆定Vp/Vs或Wadati圖法存在顯著侷限。研究發現強降雨與低含水層水位的交會可觸發約Mw 3.5的適度地震，這些主震會導致局部斷層的慢滑動與垂直平面的次生地震（主要由應力不平衡驅動），並確認盆地轉換相與圓錐相為地面震動的主要來源，建議將此類次生波相納入都市地震風險評估與建築規範。

## [2247] 2025 年 4 月 23 日土耳其西北部馬爾馬拉 Mw 6.2 地震之分布式聲學感測（DAS）觀測及與井中地震儀資料之比較
**Distributed acoustic sensing observations of the 2025 April 23 Mw 6.2 Marmara earthquake in Northwestern Türkiye and Its comparison with borehole seismometer data**

- 期刊：GJI (OUP) — RSS
- Published：Fri, 22 May 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1093/gji/ggag203
- 原文連結：[連結](https://academic.oup.com/gji/article/doi/10.1093/gji/ggag203/8691063?rss=1)

**Summary (EN)**
The study used an existing submarine telecommunication dark fibre as a long-aperture DAS array to record the 2025 April 23 Mw 6.2 Marmara earthquake near İstanbul, providing ~34 km aperture with 8 m channel spacing and 250 Hz sampling. DAS strain-rate recordings showed clear P- and S-wave arrivals across thousands of channels and, after amplitude/phase calibration and gauge-length conversion (using curvelet, f–k and time-domain slowness methods), produced particle-velocity time series consistent with nearby GONAF borehole seismometers and preserving seismic energy up to ~25 Hz. Systematic variations in apparent velocity (Vp ≈ 2.38 km/s, Vs ≈ 2.30 km/s), amplitude decay and short-wavelength strain fluctuations—attributed to shallow sediment heterogeneity and cable-related factors—were observed, demonstrating that dark-fibre infrastructure can serve as high-density, long-offset seismic arrays for near-fault strain monitoring of offshore fault segments.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
本研究將既有海底電信暗光纖作為長孔徑的DAS陣列，記錄了2025年4月23日靠近伊斯坦堡的Mw 6.2 馬爾馬拉地震，提供約34 km孔徑、8 m通道間距與250 Hz取樣。經振幅與相位校正並以量測長度轉換（採用曲波列、f–k與時域斜率方法）後，DAS應變速率資料顯示數千通道上明顯的P、S波到時，轉換後的粒子速度時序與鄰近GONAF井下地震儀相符，並可保存約25 Hz以內的地震能量。資料顯示系統性速度（Vp ≈ 2.38 km/s、Vs ≈ 2.30 km/s）與振幅衰減變化及短波長應變波動，可能反映淺層沉積差異及纜線方向/埋設條件，說明暗光纖可作為高密度、長距離的海域近斷層應變監測陣列。

## [2258] 奧克拉荷馬州與堪薩斯州誘發地震序列之應力演化分析
**Stress Evolution Analysis of Induced Seismicity Sequences in Oklahoma and Kansas**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Wed, 10 Jun 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220260077/731718
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220260077/731718/Stress-Evolution-Analysis-of-Induced-Seismicity)

**Summary (EN)**
The authors applied stress-evolution modeling to 73 induced-earthquake sequences (each with >150 events) in Oklahoma and Kansas to assess mechanisms driving fault activation. Of 24,937 earthquakes, 7,499 occurred on fault portions destressed by prior events, 6,513 occurred where prior earthquakes increased stress by >1 MPa, and the rest experienced only small stress changes; these three categories account for roughly 32%, 32%, and 36% of seismic moment, respectively. The study concludes that multiple processes drive induced seismicity and that the relative importance of those mechanisms varies across sequences.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
作者對俄克拉何馬與堪薩斯的73個誘發地震序列（每序列均超過150次事件）進行應力演化建模，以評估驅動斷層啟動的機制。在24,937次地震中，7,499次發生在先前地震已使其應力降低的斷層區段，6,513次發生在先前地震使應力增加超過1 MPa的區段，其餘則僅受小幅應力變化影響；這三類事件分別約佔總地震矩的32%、32%和36%。研究指出，誘發地震由多種過程共同驅動，且各機制的相對作用在不同序列間有所變化。
