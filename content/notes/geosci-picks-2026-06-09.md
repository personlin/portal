## [2024] 邁向具有三維低頻合成波的 SCEC 寬頻平台 (BBP 3D)
**Toward an SCEC Broadband Platform with 3D Low‐Frequency Synthetics (BBP 3D)**

- 期刊：Geophysics (GeoScienceWorld) — Advance Access
- Published：Wed, 27 May 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0120250270/731186
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/bssa/article/doi/10.1785/0120250270/731186/Toward-an-SCEC-Broadband-Platform-with-3D-Low)

**Summary (EN)**
The Broadband Platform (BBP) currently uses 1D, region-specific velocity models for low-frequency ground-motion simulations, which can miss effects from 3D sedimentary basins and complex topography and thus introduce inaccuracies. The authors implemented a BBP 3D that replaces the 1D low-frequency simulations with 3D physics-based simulations including topography and tested it on the 1989 Loma Prieta, 1996 Northridge, and 2019 Ridgecrest events using source ensembles. BBP 3D reduced the 0.2–1 Hz mean Fourier amplitude spectral (FAS) bias by as much as 89% (largest on the vertical component), produced the biggest improvements in populated basin areas (with topography explaining up to 50% of the gain), and increased goodness-of-fit for median 0.2–1 Hz duration and cumulative absolute velocity by as much as 79% and 70%, respectively, with smaller improvements between 1 and 2 Hz.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
現有的 Broadband Platform (BBP) 以一維、區域最佳化的速度模型計算低頻地動，無法完整呈現三維沉積盆地與複雜地形的影響，可能導致結果不準確。作者開發並測試了 BBP 3D，將一維低頻模擬替換為包含地形的三維物理模擬，對 1989 年 Loma Prieta、1996 年 Northridge 與 2019 年 Ridgecrest 事件進行源集成測試。BBP 3D 在 0.2–1 Hz 頻段將平均傅立葉振幅譜（FAS）偏差最多降低至 89%，垂直分量改善最大；在人口稠密的盆地區域改善最顯著（地形貢獻最多約 50%），且中位數 0.2–1 Hz 持續時間與累積絕對速度的擬合度分別提高最多約 79% 與 70%，在 1–2 Hz 頻段的改善則較小。

## [2025] 德拉瓦盆地（西德州及東南新墨西哥，美國）小地震之矩震級
**Moment Magnitude for Small Earthquakes in the Delaware Basin of West Texas and Southeast New Mexico, USA**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Wed, 27 May 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220250246/731189
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220250246/731189/Moment-Magnitude-for-Small-Earthquakes-in-the)

**Summary (EN)**
The Delaware Basin in west Texas and southeast New Mexico has become one of the most seismically active regions in the continental U.S. due to widespread hydraulic fracturing and wastewater disposal injection, creating a need for rapid and accurate earthquake source characterization. This study re-evaluates magnitudes for small earthquakes using moment-rate spectra from S‑wave coda envelopes and a relative magnitude method that uses amplitude ratios of highly correlated waveform pairs. The coda-envelope method produces accurate Mw estimates for M 1.5–3 consistent with independent Mw for larger events, and the calibrated relative-amplitude approach extended Mw,rel values to 81% of the Texas Seismological Network catalog in the Delaware Basin and 45% of the USGS Induced Seismicity Project catalog in southeast New Mexico, improving characterization of induced seismicity and the relationship between local and moment magnitude.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
德拉瓦河盆地（德州西部與新墨西哥州東南部）因廣泛的水力壓裂與廢水注入，已成為美國本土地區地震活動最頻繁的區域之一，因而需要迅速且準確的震源特性描述。本研究使用來自S波餘波包絡的矩率頻譜以及僅依賴高度相關波形對幅值比的相對震級方法，重新評估小震的震級。餘波包絡方法可對M 1.5–3的小震產生與較大事件獨立矩震級相符的準確Mw估計，經校準的相對幅值方法則將Mw,rel擴展到德拉瓦盆地德州地震網目錄的81%及新墨西哥州東南部USGS誘發地震專案目錄的45%，有助於改進誘發地震的特性描述及地方震級與矩震級之間的關係。

## [2082] 針對智利俯衝帶地震之新偽頻譜加速度（PSA）地震動模型
**A New Pseudospectral Acceleration Ground‐Motion Model for Subduction Zone Earthquakes in Chile**

- 期刊：Geophysics (GeoScienceWorld) — Advance Access
- Published：Fri, 29 May 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0120250242/731318
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/bssa/article/doi/10.1785/0120250242/731318/A-New-Pseudospectral-Acceleration-Ground-Motion)

**Summary (EN)**
The paper presents new ground‑motion models (GMMs) for pseudospectral accelerations of interface and intraslab earthquakes in Chile, developed from an extensive strong‑motion dataset spanning a wide range of magnitudes, distances, and site conditions. A data–physics hybrid regression is used to constrain poorly sampled large‑magnitude, short‑distance scaling, producing two model versions bounded by NGA‑Sub South American and NGA‑Sub global limits to represent epistemic uncertainty. The models better reproduce scaling for small‑to‑moderate events, include a Chile site‑amplification term, reduce aleatory variability (with a single‑station sigma ≈0.45 ln units), and reveal spatial and interperiod residual patterns that motivate future nonergodic studies; together they offer a regionally calibrated, physically consistent toolset for seismic hazard, design, and risk assessment and a transferable approach for data‑sparse regions.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
本研究為智利俯衝帶（界面與盤內）地震建立新的譜加速度地動模型，所用強震資料涵蓋廣泛的震級、距離與場址條件。採用數據與物理相結合的回歸方法，對智利資料稀少的大震短距離外推進行約束，並推得兩個以 NGA‑Sub（南美與全球）邊界約束的模型版本以表示認識上的不確定性。新模型可更好描述中小震的地動標度，導出與其他地區一致的智利場址放大項，並透過單站 sigma（約0.45 ln）等方法減少隨機變異；空間場址項與跨週期殘差關係也指出需發展非均質（nonergodic）處理，這些模型與回歸框架為智利的震害評估、耐震設計與風險分析提供了區域化且具物理一致性的基礎，且方法可移植至資料不足的其他地區。

## [2091] 伊斯坦堡電信光纖的淺層建模與場址放大
**Shallow Modeling and Site‐Amplification From Telecommunications Fibers in Istanbul**

- 期刊：JGR: Solid Earth (AGU/Wiley) — eTOC
- Published：Sat, 30 May 2026 00:17:53 -0700
- DOI：10.1029/2025GL121011
- 原文連結：[連結](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2025GL121011?af=R)

**Summary (EN)**
The authors use pre-existing fiber-optic telecommunications cables in Istanbul to derive a shallow seismic velocity model down to 100 m by recording ambient urban-noise Rayleigh waves and inverting dispersion curves with a trans-dimensional Monte Carlo algorithm. They estimate site amplification three ways: 1D profiles from the velocity model, 2D earthquake simulations across the model, and comparison with observed earthquake records dominated by the 2023 Kahramanmaraş sequence. All three approaches yield similar spatial patterns and amplification factors, demonstrating that urban telecom fibers can be effective for seismic hazard and site-response estimation.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
作者利用伊斯坦布爾的既有光纖通訊線路，透過記錄以城市交通為主的環境雜訊並提取瑞利波色散曲線，建立向下至100公尺的淺層地震速度模型，並以跨維度蒙地卡羅反演估算剪切波速度。研究以三種方式估算場址放大：由速度模型導出的1D估計、基於模型的2D地震模擬，以及與以2023年卡赫拉曼馬拉什序列為主的實際地震資料比較。三種方法在放大空間分布與放大因子上大致一致，顯示在都市環境中使用通訊光纖進行地震危害評估的可行性。
