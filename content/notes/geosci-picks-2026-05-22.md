## [1909] 台灣井下分布式聲學感測偵測到的閃電誘發深部內爆式微震事件
**Lightning‐Induced Implosive Microseismic Events at Depth Detected by Downhole Distributed Acoustic Sensing in Taiwan**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Tue, 19 May 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220250432/730900
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220250432/730900/Lightning-Induced-Implosive-Microseismic-Events-at)

**Summary (EN)**
The authors report detection of impulsive seismic waveforms by a downhole distributed acoustic sensing (DAS) system in a borehole in Taiwan immediately following a cloud‑to‑ground lightning strike, providing direct evidence for subsurface energy dissipation from lightning. The recorded strain‑rate waveforms show a single distinct seismic phase with upward moveouts between DAS channels at 50–86 m depth and downward moveouts between 86–140 m, peak strain rates of ~3×10−6 s−1, and strong attenuation during propagation. Finite‑difference strain‑rate simulations of the source mechanism indicate the waves were produced by an implosive event of estimated magnitude Mw −1.5 to −1.0 (Mxx=Myy=Mzz).

**重點摘要（繁中）**
作者報告在台灣井中部署的井下分布式聲學感測（DAS）系統，於一場雲對地閃電擊後即偵測到脈衝性地震波，提供閃電在地下能量耗散的直接證據。量測到的應變速率波形僅含一個明顯地震相，在50–86公尺間呈向上走時差，在86–140公尺間呈向下走時差，最大應變速率約為3×10−6 s−1，且波幅傳播時衰減明顯。利用有限差分應變速率模擬分析之來源機制顯示，此波為一個估計規模Mw −1.5至−1.0的內爆事件（Mxx=Myy=Mzz）。

## [1910] 以低成本離散斷層模擬重現2019年 Ridgecrest 地震破裂
**2019 Ridgecrest Earthquake Rupture Reproduced by Low‐Cost Discrete Fault Simulation**

- 期刊：Seismological Research Letters (SRL) — Advance Access
- Published：Tue, 19 May 2026 00:00:00 GMT
- DOI：10.1785/0220250380/730899
- 原文連結：[連結](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/ssa/srl/article/doi/10.1785/0220250380/730899/2019-Ridgecrest-Earthquake-Rupture-Reproduced-by)

**Summary (EN)**
We developed a simplified, low‑cost earthquake simulator using a pixel‑tracing technique to generate 3D nonplanar fault geometry for the 2019 Ridgecrest sequence, assuming fault geometry is the only source of stress. Running on a standard desktop (Intel 13900k) with grid sizes of 200 m and 500 m produced the sequence in about 20 minutes and 1 minute respectively. The simulations sometimes reproduce the observed foreshock–mainshock order and produce slip distributions and magnitudes comparable to measurements (and similar between grid sizes), although timing and rupture sequencing are highly sensitive to stress inputs and numerical choices; overall the method is a useful tool for probing rupture mechanisms and rapid scenario testing in complex fault systems.

**重點摘要（繁中）**
我們開發了一種簡化的低成本地震模擬器，利用像素追蹤技術生成2019年Ridgecrest地震的三維非平面斷層幾何，並假設斷層幾何是唯一的應力來源。於標準桌機（Intel 13900k）上以200 m與500 m格網模擬，分別約需20分鐘及1分鐘即可產生地震序列。模擬在某些情況下能重現觀測到的前震－主震順序並產生與測量相近的滑動分佈與規模，但時序與破裂過程對應力設定與數值方案高度敏感；整體而言，此方法對研究特定破裂機制及在複雜斷層幾何下快速模擬潛在地震具實用價值。
